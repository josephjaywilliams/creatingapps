# One Minute

**Pitch:** One tiny action a day, by email. That's the whole commitment.

**LinkedIn draft:**
> Most health apps ask for weeks of commitment. Most people quit within days.
> So my lab asked: what's the smallest thing that still helps?
> One minute. One action. One email a day.
> In our 14-day study, people completed 82% of the prompts.
> Try it free for two weeks: https://josephjaywilliams.github.io/creatingapps/oneminapp1/
> (Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29051)

**Send to:** busy professionals and students who "don't have time" for wellness, managers looking for
low-effort team well-being ideas, behavior-change and HCI researchers, health coaches, the paper's co-authors.
